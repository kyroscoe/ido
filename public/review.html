Christyn’s review guide

A few details before
we say “I do.”

This is a separate design preview. The existing website has not been edited. All rewritten first-person copy is proposed for your approval.

Content & service decisions

  • Experience: confirm the existing “7+ years” claim is still accurate.
  • Coordination: confirm the handover lead time, preparation meetings, rehearsal coverage, wedding-day hours, vendor communication, setup and cleanup limits, and staffing.
  • Planning: Initial Consultation, Comprehensive Event Planning, and Day-of Coordination are included from the existing Services page. Confirm detailed planning deliverables, consultation format, and package boundaries; venue selection, decor design, and vendor coordination are reflected in the planning overview.
  • Packages & pricing: provide approved names, inclusions, starting prices (if public), travel fees, and booking terms.
  • Other events & travel: confirm non-wedding event types and the area served beyond Central Ohio.
  • Voice & process: approve the rewritten personal copy and proposed inquiry → conversation → coordination process. No reply-time promise or booking terms have been invented.

Photography & testimonials

  • The ceremony, portrait, reception, skyline, and logo assets come from the existing site. Confirm permission to reuse them, photographer credits, and any image-specific requirements.
  • Kayla and Ben’s quote is an exact excerpt from the existing homepage. Confirm continued permission to feature their names and testimonial.
  • No wedding feature has been invented. Supply an approved photo set tied to a named wedding, permission, credits, and a brief account of what you handled to add one.
  • Supply the verified Instagram and Facebook profile URLs. The existing Wix social links have been omitted.

Inquiry & launch requirements

  • Confirm hello@idocoordinationandevents.com as the form destination and approve an expected response time. The email and phone shown in the preview are from the current site.
  • Approve a test destination, connect the form to a delivery service, then verify delivery, reply routing, spam handling, and failure messages before launch. This preview validates fields only; it sends and stores nothing.
  • Confirm the inquiry follow-up wording, privacy policy, data retention, and any consent wording for the final form. No newsletter signup or analytics is active.
  • Approve the final design and copy before moving it to the production domain. Keep the live site as-is until launch is explicitly authorized.

The current editorial revision includes responsive phone, tablet, and desktop styles.
