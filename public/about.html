Meet Christyn Martin

Heartfelt care for your special day.

Christyn is the owner of I Do Coordination & Events. With 7+ years of experience coordinating weddings throughout Central Ohio, she leads our work with organization, professionalism, and genuine care.

We believe every couple deserves to be fully present on one of the most important days of their lives. From managing timelines and coordinating vendors to handling unexpected challenges, we help your plans come together so you can focus on making lifelong memories.

Christyn’s faith shapes our commitment to honesty, kindness, and servant-hearted leadership. We care for every wedding as we would our own family’s celebration.
